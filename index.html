About

I study nonlinear dynamics and complex networks, with an emphasis on synchronization, higher-order interactions, and physics-informed learning. My work connects spectral methods and dynamical systems with questions of network robustness, control, and nonequilibrium statistical physics.

I joined the PhD program in Complex Networks at Fudan University in 2024. I received my M.Sc. in Theoretical Physics from East China Normal University in 2024.

Research Interests

  • Nonlinear dynamics
  • Complex networks
  • Synchronization
  • Reservoir computing
  • Quantum dynamics
  • Higher-order interactions
  • Stochastic thermodynamics
  • Physics-informed learning

Methods. Spectral perturbation theory, Kuramoto–Sakaguchi and Stuart–Landau models, Fokker–Planck and probability-current methods, Lyapunov and bifurcation analysis, and physics-informed neural and variational optimization.
